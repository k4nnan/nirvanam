From the Chettiar mansions to your home

The Chettiar merchants built grand mansions across Tamil Nadu, filled with carved teak doors, brass lamps, enamel ware and furniture made to last generations. As the houses are dismantled, much of what they held is lost or sold by weight.

We travel to Chettinad and choose each piece ourselves, in small numbers. It is cleaned, carefully crated, and sent to you anywhere in the world, with its story and the name of its wood.

The profit does two things. It pays to bring tired antique furniture back to life, and it plants trees: a woodland in west Wales and a food forest in South India.

Then, once the collection is established, we will release our own signature furniture line, finished with hand-set brass inlay.
