Heritage furniture revived for a second life

We buy hundred-year-old Burma teak from the dismantled mansions of Chettinad and rebuild it as furniture, with every imperfection filled in brass. Each piece is one of one, and carries the name of the person who made it.

Where the wood comes from

The Chettiar mansions of Tamil Nadu were built from Burma teak and lit by brass. Many have been taken down piece by piece, and their timber sold by weight.

We buy that timber and give it another hundred years. We don't restore it to look new. Splits and losses are bridged in brass, so you can see exactly where the wood has been.

The Welsh line follows: hardwood from our own thirteen acres in Ceredigion, thinned, milled and seasoned there.
