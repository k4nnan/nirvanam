2. What we collect and why

Joining the list (first refusal)

When you join the list we collect your email address. With it we record when you signed up, which form you used, the page you were on, the headline you saw, how you reached the site (for example a campaign link or referring website), your browser type, and the "interest" option you pick, if any. If you accepted analytics cookies, we also record your anonymous Google Analytics ID (see section 4).

We use this to send you news of Nirvanam pieces and launches, to offer first refusal to the first hundred people, and to understand which pages and campaigns bring people to the list.

Lawful basis: your consent. You can unsubscribe from any email, or ask us to remove you, at any time.

Registering interest in a piece

When you register interest in a piece in the Chettinad Collection we collect your name, email address, country and any note you write, along with the piece, the page, how you reached the site and, if you accepted analytics cookies, your anonymous Google Analytics ID.

We use this to contact you about that piece, its price and delivery.

Lawful basis: taking steps at your request before entering into a contract with you.

Emailing us

If you email us, we keep the message and your contact details so we can reply. Lawful basis: our legitimate interest in answering enquiries.

Using the website

Our host keeps standard server logs, which include your IP address, for security and to keep the site running. If you accept analytics cookies we also measure how the site is used (see section 3).

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

3. Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics 4, loaded through Google Tag Manager, to understand how people use the site so we can improve it. Neither loads, and no analytics cookie is set, unless you click "Accept" on the cookie banner. If you decline or ignore the banner, nothing from Google Analytics or Google Tag Manager runs.

What we measure when you accept

  • Pages viewed, how far you scroll, and how long you stay.
  • The links, buttons and tabs you click (their label, where on the page they are and, for links, where they go), and whether you join the list or register interest in a piece.
  • Your approximate location (country and city), derived from your IP address. Google Analytics 4 does not store IP addresses.
  • Your device, browser, screen size and language, and the website or campaign that brought you here.

We never send your name or email address to Google Analytics. Advertising features are switched off: we do not use this data for advertising, remarketing or ad personalisation.

Lawful basis: your consent, as required by the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR) for non-essential cookies.

Changing your mind

You can change your choice at any time. Use the button below or the "Cookies" link at the bottom of every page. If you withdraw consent, we stop measuring your visits and delete the Google Analytics cookies from your browser.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install Google's Analytics opt-out add-on.

4. Linking your visits to your signup

Google Analytics identifies your browser by a random ID in the _ga cookie. It does not identify you by name. If you have accepted analytics cookies and then join the list or register interest in a piece, we save that random ID with your signup in our own records. We also send Google Analytics a random reference number for the signup (never your email address).

This lets us see, in our own copy of the analytics data, which pages and campaigns led to signups. It also means the analytics records linked to your signup are personal data, and we treat them that way: they are covered by your rights in section 8, and we will delete them on request. If you declined cookies, there is no ID to link, so nothing is linked.

5. Who we share data with

We use these service providers. They process data for us under contract, on our instructions:

  • Google (Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC): Google Analytics 4 and Google Tag Manager for analytics; Google BigQuery, where we keep a copy of our analytics data in Google Cloud's London region (europe-west2); Google Sheets and Google Apps Script, which hold our mailing list and enquiries; and Google Fonts, which serves the site's typefaces (so your browser makes a request to Google, including your IP address, when it loads a page).
  • GitHub (GitHub, Inc.): hosts this website and keeps server logs.

Links to Instagram, Pinterest and YouTube take you to those sites, which have their own privacy policies. We may also share data where the law requires it, or to protect our rights.

6. International transfers

Google and GitHub may process data in the United States and other countries outside the UK. Where they do, the transfer is protected by the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (where the provider is certified) or by the UK International Data Transfer Addendum to the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. How long we keep it

  • Mailing list: until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove you. After that we keep only your email address on a suppression list, so we never email you by mistake.
  • Interest in a piece: up to 2 years after our last contact, unless you buy from us. Purchase records are kept for 6 years for tax and accounting.
  • Google Analytics: detailed data is kept for 14 months in Google Analytics, then deleted. Summary reports with no individual data may be kept longer.
  • Our analytics copy in BigQuery: 26 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Server logs: as set by our host, usually no more than 90 days.

8. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct data that is wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your data;
  • ask us to restrict how we use it, or object to our use of it;
  • receive data you gave us in a portable format;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting anything we did before you withdrew it.

To use any of these rights, email hello@nirvanamstudio.com. We will reply within one month. There is normally no charge.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, please tell us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

9. Changes to this notice

We will update this notice when we change how we use personal data, and change the date at the top. If a change affects how we use data you have already given us, we will tell you by email before it takes effect.
